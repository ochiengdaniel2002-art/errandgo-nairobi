Privacy Policy

How ErrandGo Nairobi handles personal information when you contact us or request our services.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Introduction

ErrandGo Nairobi respects your privacy. This policy explains how we may collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us or request an errand or delivery service.

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection laws in Kenya, including the Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details, such as your phone number.
  • Pickup and delivery locations or other directions you provide.
  • Details about the errand, items or service you request.
  • Messages and information you choose to send us through WhatsApp or other communication channels.
  • Basic technical information that may be recorded by website hosting services, such as browser or access information.

Please provide only information needed to arrange your request. Avoid sending passwords, PINs, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through ordinary messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests.
  • Confirm errand details, availability and service fees.
  • Coordinate pickups, purchases and deliveries.
  • Communicate with you about the progress of a request.
  • Maintain appropriate service records and address complaints or disputes.
  • Protect the website and help prevent misuse or fraud.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

Some website buttons may open WhatsApp so that you can send us your request. When you use WhatsApp, your information is also handled under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub and other service providers may process certain technical information when you access the website. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

We do not control the privacy practices or security of third-party platforms. Please review their policies before using their services.

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share information when reasonably necessary to fulfil your request, for example with a relevant service provider involved in a pickup or delivery. We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to address security issues or disputes.

We aim to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Storage and Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service coordination, record keeping, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. The appropriate period depends on the type of information and the circumstances.

Information held in WhatsApp or by website hosting providers may also be subject to those providers' retention practices.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no website, messaging service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, ask for incorrect information to be corrected, request deletion where applicable, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may contact us using the details below to make a privacy-related request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website may use essential technical features needed for it to function. If analytics or other tracking tools are added, this policy should be updated to explain what they collect and how you can manage your choices.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for people who can lawfully request them. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information unnecessarily. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, website features or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
