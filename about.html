About ErrandGo

We give you back your time.

ErrandGo exists for one simple reason: life is too busy to spend your day running from one place to another.

Our Story

Less running around. More living.

Kenya moves fast. Between work, business, family and everything else life throws at you, even simple errands can consume hours of your day.

ErrandGo was created to make those everyday tasks easier. Whether you need something picked up, delivered, purchased or handled, you can tell us what needs doing and let us take care of the rest.

We're building a service around convenience, reliability and communication — because when you trust someone with your errand, you deserve to know what's happening every step of the way.
